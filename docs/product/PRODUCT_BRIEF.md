# Wipeband product brief

Updated: 2026-10-28. Based on the confirmed assessment and existing site inputs.

For: Parents and caregivers seeking tissues within children's reach during
school, play, and travel; schools/groups are a secondary inquiry audience.

Problem: Children need a tissue while a box or bag may be elsewhere, leading
to sleeve-wiping or interruptions to activities.

Promise: A reusable wrist-worn band keeps replaceable tissue layers nearby.
Explain the practical reason for wearing it and the wear/wipe/tear routine.

Core loop: Understand the product, choose a pattern, buy a starter kit directly,
use and replace tissue layers, then reorder refills. Amazon is an alternative.

Market: Everyday children's hygiene convenience. Tissue boxes, loose tissues,
and packs carried in bags are functional alternatives; no competitor research
or market-size claims have been validated in this adoption session.

Current bets: Preserve the existing light-theme storefront and client revisions;
make direct purchase clear; verify trust/commerce behavior before release.
Next: prototype improved storefront motion and a small admin order workflow.
Keep core wristband/refill products central; automatic subscriptions are not
part of the request. The existing public Wipeband.com site is being redeveloped.
Owner correction: preserve the existing storefront content, products, prices
and section order in visual mockups. Improve presentation with considered
texture/pattern/artwork and purposeful motion. Landing page first; the admin
requires a separate, deeper workflow study rather than a bare scaffold.

Success signals: To be agreed with the owner. Candidate checks are a clear
product explanation, reliable pattern-to-checkout flow, and honest support/
tracking states. No conversion target or analytics setup is assumed.

Not doing: A stack migration, broad CMS/marketplace backend, unsolicited
deployment, subscription billing, or final visual asset generation. Images are
supplied by the owner and stored in-repo. Minimal operational storage for
orders/fulfillment/retries is approved; provider selection is pending.

Rules: Keep copy/claims grounded in approved evidence; verify age, materials,
patent, shipping/returns and review permissions. The original fact checklist
remains unresolved. Preserve the existing UI while reconciling client feedback.

Operations: GitHub pushes trigger Hostinger deployments. Vercel preview testing
is planned by the owner; preview URL is pending. Shared Claude/Codex docs are
the durable project record.

Order operations: customer pays on the site; verified payment initiates the
Amazon MCF handoff; admin sees orders, tracking and actionable exceptions.
Customer confirmation and tracking emails only; merchant monitors admin.
Contact/bulk inquiries are delivered to the existing inbox. Amazon MCF/API or
connector readiness must be assessed with the owner before implementation.

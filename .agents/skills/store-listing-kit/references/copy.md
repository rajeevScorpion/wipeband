# Listing copy method

## Slide headlines
- One benefit per slide, the user's outcome rather than the feature name: "Reminders that actually remind you." beats "Reminder settings".
- 2–6 words, max 2 lines at ~92 px. Full stop at the end reads confident and premium.
- One `emphasis` word in the accent colour: the word that carries the promise ("say", "whole", "calm").
- Subline: one plain sentence (≤ 60 chars) that says how. Name the product as the actor ("WowTodo turns your words into a to-do list") instead of the technology behind it.
- Order: 1 core promise → 2 the "wow" moment → 3 the habit/retention feature → 4 differentiator (language, privacy, offline…) → 5–6 depth → last: polish (dark mode, widgets) or trust.
- Localized headline for a localized market is a strong signal (e.g. a Hindi headline slide for India). Use the script's own font.

## Short description (≤ 80)
Formula: `<promise>. <Product> <does what> <for whom/with what>. <differentiator>.` Count exactly; aim for 70–80.
Example (WowTodo): "Just say it. WowTodo turns your words into tasks and reminders. Hindi & English."

## Full description (≤ 4000; 1500–2500 is plenty)
1. Hook line (the slide-1 promise), then 2–3 sentences with one concrete example in quotes, the way a user would actually say/do it.
2. A one-line contrast ("No forms. No fiddly date pickers.").
3. 4–7 feature blocks: CAPS heading (the benefit) + 1–2 sentences. Mirror the slide order so the listing feels coherent.
4. Trust block: sign-in method, ads (none?), data deletion, a plain privacy sentence. Technical disclosures (third-party processing, AI services) go here and only here if the project prefers not to lead with them.
5. Permissions: one line each, why it's needed.
6. Requirements (internet, OS) last.

## Tone and banned patterns
- Plain, warm, specific. Short sentences. Second person.
- No superlatives or rankings, no prices/promos, no "Download now", no emoji walls, no keyword lists, no claims about untested features.
- Respect project wording rules (e.g. keep "AI" out of headlines and the short description if the owner wants that).
- Fictional examples should match the market (local names, places, currency).

## Feature graphic
Logo + wordmark, the slide-1 promise as the headline (≤ 5 words), one subline (≤ 6 words), a phone showing the fullest screen, optionally one callout. Nothing important in the outer 10%.

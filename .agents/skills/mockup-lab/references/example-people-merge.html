WowTodo · People tab proposal

One People tab for contacts and everything you share

The Shared tab goes away. Contacts, requests and every shared task live under People, and each card says who it's from in plain words. The phone on this page is a working mockup with sample people and tasks. Tap through it; nothing is saved and no real accounts are involved.

What changes

  1. One place for sharing

    Before
    Shared tab (For Me / Shared) and People tab (contacts) showed the same shares twice.
    After
    People holds contacts, requests and every shared task. The dock loses the Shared tab.

  2. What's waiting for you comes first

    Before
    Incoming shares sat in Shared › For Me; contact requests sat in People.
    After
    "Needs your answer" at the top of People, with Peek, Decline and Accept right on the card.

  3. Direction is in the words

    Before
    "Their Tasks / Your Tasks" read like ownership, not who shared with whom.
    After
    "From Riya / To Riya". Every card starts with "From Riya" or "You → Riya".

  4. The right face on each card

    Before
    Your own task showed the other person's photo, so it looked like theirs.
    After
    From cards show the sender. To cards show you, then them.

  5. Status and Stop sharing on the card

    Before
    Status and Revoke lived only on the Shared tab.
    After
    Waiting, Accepted or Declined on every To card, with Stop sharing. A declined share shows their note.

  6. Safety actions move into ⋯

    Before
    Block, Report and Remove contact sat above the tasks, in the most prominent spot.
    After
    In the ⋯ menu on the person's page, each with a confirm step.

  7. Share from the person

    Before
    Sharing started only from a task.
    After
    "Share a task with Riya" on their page picks from your tasks. Sharing from a task still works.

  8. Optional: a By task view

    Before
    —
    After
    All shared tasks in one list: Waiting for you, Shared with you, Shared by you. Useful once someone shares with many people.

Decide before we build

  • Tab labels on a person's page"From Riya / To Riya" as shown, or "Shared with me / Shared by me".
  • Dock3 tabs (shown first): Tasks · mic · People, with Settings moving into the ☰ menu. Or 5 tabs: the bell moves into the freed slot as Alerts. Switch it above the phone.
  • By task viewKeep it as a second view, or leave People person-only.
  • Peek before acceptingKeep Peek as a sheet (shown), or open the full task read-only.

Build notes

  • No database change. The screen regroups the share and contact data the app already loads.
  • Existing pieces are reused: share cards, the decline-with-note sheet, block and report dialogs, contact rows, Add contact.
  • All People copy moves into one English + Hindi file. Today the Shared and person screens are English only.
  • The old Shared route stays as a redirect, so taps on older notifications still land somewhere sensible.
  • Branch sharing is on hold. The "1 branch" chip on a card is a placeholder.

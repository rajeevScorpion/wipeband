Say it the way it comes to you.

No forms, no fields to fill. Speak or type one sentence, in English, in Hindi, or in the mix most of us actually use. “Kal shaam 6 baje” becomes 6 pm tomorrow. “Plan Meera's birthday on Saturday” becomes a plan with steps.

Nothing is saved until you've seen it. WowTodo shows you the task first, so you can change a word or a time.

What changes

  • Copy and story: approved (copy deck draft 1).
  • Live demo gets its own section (Capture); the hero keeps “Try it here”.
  • Email box in the hero too, sharing one form logic with the closing form.
  • No Diwali strip.

Build notes

  • Reused as is: tokens, fonts, PhoneFrame, the 4 interactive demos (names swapped to fictional Indian names), live demo panel, theme toggle.
  • New components: Moments, Midpoint line, Stories cards, Early-access form + questions, FAQ, Diwali strip. Each section reads its words from one content file, not hard-coded.
  • Removed: “Calm” interlude, 1–6 numbering (two “3”s today), Play badge CTA, footer line “intelligent execution companion…”.
  • Backend: the waitlist function and table being built now (S2): confirm email, optional questions, remove link. No analytics.
  • Screens to capture: review screen with clash heads-up + repeat chip; group Progress tab (after your phone check). Light + dark.
  • Motion: content is visible at rest; light entrances and the idea rotation only. Reduced-motion turns them off.
  • Out of scope here: /stories pages (S5), search-engine pre-rendering (S6).

# Privacy policy lines (adapt to the project's sections; plain words)

- **Data we collect:** if you turn on "Email me updates" (Settings → Notifications), we keep that choice and when you
  made it. If you reply to one of our emails, we keep your reply (sender, subject, text) so the team can read and
  answer it.
- **How we use it:** service emails about your account, test or place on the list; update emails only if you said
  yes, each with an unsubscribe link. Emails come from hello@<domain>; replies reach the team.
- **Who processes it:** our email provider (e.g. Resend) sends our emails and receives replies, then forwards a copy
  to the team inbox (e.g. Zoho).
- **Retention:** replies and the record of emails we sent are deleted with your account. We keep a short list of
  addresses that unsubscribed, bounced or reported spam — only the address and the reason — so we never email them
  again, even after the account is deleted.
- **Waitlist / early access:** the same unsubscribe and reply rules apply to waitlist emails.
- Store data-safety form: no new data type usually (email address is declared); add the "Developer communications"
  purpose if update emails are new.

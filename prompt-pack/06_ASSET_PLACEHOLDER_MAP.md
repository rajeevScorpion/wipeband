## Placeholder appearance

Use a quiet brand-tinted surface with:
- thin border
- subtle icon/shape
- small monospace asset filename
- no fake photograph
- no fake product
- no random stock image

Hide the filename label in production if desired, but preserve the DOM/data attribute or code comment.

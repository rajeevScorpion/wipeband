## 13. FAQ Preview

# Frequently asked questions

### What exactly is Wipeband?

Wipeband is a reusable, watch-like band that keeps a layered tissue pad conveniently on a child's wrist.

### How does my child use it?

Wear it like a watch, wipe using the soft tissue layer, then remove the used layer when finished.

### Can I reuse the band?

Yes. The Wipeband itself is reusable. When the tissue pack is finished, replace it with a fresh refill pack.

### Where can Wipeband be used?

It is designed for everyday situations such as school, travel, playdates and time on the go.

### Do you sell replacement tissues?

Yes. Wipeband refill packs are available separately.

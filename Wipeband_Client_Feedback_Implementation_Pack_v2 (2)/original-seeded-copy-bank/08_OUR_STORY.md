## 02. The Everyday Problem

# Little noses do not wait for convenient moments.

Kids are busy.

They are learning, playing, traveling, running between activities and often thinking about everything except where the tissues are.

A tissue box may be across the room.

A tissue packet may be buried in a bag.

A sleeve is always close.

That simple mismatch became the starting point for Wipeband.

---

## 03. The Idea

# What if the tissue stayed where the child naturally reaches?

Instead of asking children to stop and search for a tissue, the idea was to move the tissue closer to them.

Right onto the wrist.

That became Wipeband:

a reusable, watch-like band with a refillable layered tissue pad.

# Recommended Policy Structure

## 1. Who We Are

Identify the legal entity operating Wipeband and the website.

## 2. Information We Collect

Potential categories to review:
- account/contact information
- order information
- billing/payment-related information handled by payment processors
- shipping information
- customer-support messages
- email marketing opt-ins
- website analytics
- cookies and similar technologies

## 3. How We Use Information

Potential purposes:
- process orders
- fulfill and deliver purchases
- provide support
- communicate transactional updates
- improve the website
- manage marketing where consent/legitimate basis applies
- prevent fraud and abuse
- meet legal obligations

## 4. WooCommerce / WordPress

Describe the website platform and any relevant data handling.

## 5. Payments

Identify the actual payment processors used.

Do not claim Wipeband stores full card details unless that is true.

## 6. Amazon / MCF / Fulfillment Providers

If Amazon Multi-Channel Fulfillment is used, disclose the relevant sharing of order/fulfillment information in legally appropriate language.

## 7. Analytics & Advertising

Review:
- Google Analytics / GA4
- Meta Pixel
- Meta Conversions API
- Google Ads tags
- other tracking tools actually implemented

## 8. Email & Marketing

Describe:
- opt-in
- unsubscribe
- campaign providers
- abandoned-cart communications if used

## 9. Cookies

Include cookie categories and consent choices according to actual implementation and applicable law.

## 10. Data Sharing

Identify real processors and service providers.

## 11. Data Retention

Define retention approach.

## 12. Your Privacy Rights

Tailor to applicable U.S. state/federal rules and customer geography.

## 13. Children’s Privacy

This requires careful legal review because the product is intended for children but the website purchaser is generally an adult.

Do not improvise COPPA-related representations.

## 14. Security

Use accurate, non-absolute language.

## 15. Changes to This Policy

Add update process and effective date.

## 16. Contact

Add verified privacy contact information.

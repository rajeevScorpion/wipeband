## 02. The Core Idea

# Kids do not always stop to look for a tissue.

At school, during play, on a trip or in the middle of a busy day, a tissue box may be somewhere else.

Wipeband changes one simple thing:

**where the tissue is.**

Instead of expecting kids to remember where to find one, Wipeband keeps it on the wrist — the place they can naturally reach.

## 03. Wear It. Wipe It. Tear It.

# Three simple steps.

### 01 — Wear it.

Put Wipeband on like a watch.

The adjustable band keeps the tissue pad comfortably within reach.

### 02 — Wipe it.

Use the soft tissue layer when needed.

No searching through a bag. No leaving the activity to find a tissue box.

### 03 — Tear it.

Remove the used layer to reveal the next fresh one.

Then keep going.

# Wipeband Copy Style Guide

**Status:** WORKING MASTER

---

## Brand Voice

Wipeband should sound:

- warm
- clear
- practical
- optimistic
- parent-aware
- child-friendly without sounding childish
- engineered without sounding clinical

---

## Sentence Style

Prefer:
- short sentences
- clear verbs
- benefit-led phrasing
- concrete everyday situations
- simple explanations

Avoid:
- dense product jargon
- long inventor-story exposition on conversion pages
- exaggerated emotion
- fear-based disease messaging
- unsupported medical claims
- generic CTA labels

---

## Preferred CTA Language

Use:
- Shop Wipeband
- Shop Starter Kit
- Shop Refills
- See how it works
- Our story
- View FAQs
- Contact Support
- Track Order
- Ask About Bulk Orders

Avoid:
- Learn More
- Click Here
- Submit
- Discover More

---

## Messaging Hierarchy

### Primary benefit

A tissue is within reach.

### Practical result

Less searching. Less reliance on a nearby tissue box. Less temptation to use a sleeve.

### Product mechanism

Wearable, layered, tear-away, refillable.

### Emotional benefit

More everyday independence for kids and a simpler routine for parents.

---

## Signature Phrases

Use consistently:

**No more messy sleeves.**

**Wear it. Wipe it. Tear it.**

**Designed for kids. Built for real life.**

**Keep the band. Refresh the tissues.**

**A tissue is useful when it’s within reach.**

---

## Claims Guardrails

Do not use unverified claims such as:
- prevents colds
- prevents infection
- stops germs
- stops disease spread
- medically proven
- clinically proven
- hypoallergenic
- antibacterial
- leakproof
- germ-proof
- safe for all ages

Only use technical or safety claims supported by real documentation.

---

## Audience Balance

Remember:

**Parents buy Wipeband. Kids use Wipeband.**

Therefore:

Child-facing visual language:
- fun
- movement
- confidence
- everyday independence

Parent-facing copy:
- convenience
- portability
- simplicity
- refillability
- value
- easy product understanding

Product-facing visuals:
- clean
- engineered
- credible
- close-up
- well-lit

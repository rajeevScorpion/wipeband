## Material deviations or issues to inspect

1. **Two equal-weight commerce choices** appear repeatedly (Buy Direct alongside Amazon). Client wants direct purchase dominant, Amazon a small tertiary text link.
2. **Hero does not name runny noses explicitly**; currently `No more messy sleeves.` plus generic tissue-pad description.
3. **First screen has competing benefits** (`Cleaner kids`, `Happier parents`, `Anytime anywhere`) before the primary product explanation.
4. **3-step explanation is too low** for a new category; shorten, elevate and add what happens to the used tissue.
5. **Site has an apparently configured Family Bundle** ($44.95; three bands and fifteen packs), despite earlier copy bank marking bundle SKU and mechanics as unverified. Client now wants bundle removed from immediate shopping, because operations are not ready. Check checkout/product catalog and suspend unsafe sale until verified, without deleting useful code.
6. **Site presents product/operations facts not verified by the historical checklist:** `ages 2 and up` and `toddlers through pre-teens`; specific silicone/skin claims, cleaning instructions, 1–2 business day processing and 3–7 day delivery, 30-day returns, patent number, variant inventory availability. These may be correct, but source confirmation is required, not silent reuse.
7. **Named/testimonial verification:** `Henry M.` quote appears, and shop shows `Sarah K.` with `Verified Family Buyer` and review text. Do not publish review verification badges unless matching records/permission exist.
8. **Some copy adds unsupported/emphatic claims** like `hygienic`, `skin-safe`, `gentle on skin`, `best value`, `special family savings` and `keep the band forever`. Recheck substantiation.
9. **Refills uses engineered language** (`Engineered specifically for all Wipeband versions`) that conflicts with client's warmer preferred wording.
10. **Founder story is abbreviated**, while client wants original site backstory, introduction of Sachin and Tiana as co-inventors, and a link to Tiana's site.
11. **Current legal/policy pages have operational assertions** absent from approved legal review; retain functionality but flag for client/legal approval.
